# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 28-10-2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used by Parashift Technologies B.V., operating under the Growth Rocket brand ("Growth Rocket", "we"), on getgrowthrocket.com and in the Growth Rocket Hub (hub.getgrowthrocket.com), and how you control them. Our Privacy Policy explains the related uses of personal data. Customer websites are addressed separately in section 5.

# 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage and tracking scripts, work in comparable ways. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

# 2. Your choice

- When you first visit getgrowthrocket.com, our banner lets you accept all optional cookies, reject them all, or choose by category. Optional categories are off until you choose them. Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and you can use the website without agreeing to analytics or advertising.

- **Before you consent, and if you reject, we use only technologies needed to provide the requested service or transmit communications. Our analytics and advertising tools remain blocked.No Google or Microsoft scripts load and no data is sent to them before you consent.**

- You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through "Cookie settings", as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal stops further consent-based use but does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Where relevant, we also communicate withdrawal to the providers concerned. Your choice is remembered for 6 months; after that we ask again. The 'Cookie settings' link is available at the bottom of every page. When you withdraw consent, the affected analytics or advertising tools stop loading immediately, and we delete their cookies that are set on our domain (such as _ga, _clck and _gcl_au). Cookies set on the providers' own domains (such as Microsoft's MUID) can only be removed through your browser settings.

- Consent is not required for storage or access strictly necessary to provide a service you request, or solely to transmit a communication, under Article 11.7a of the Dutch Telecommunicatiewet and any applicable equivalent rules. Calling a cookie "functional" does not itself make it exempt. We ask for consent for the analytics and marketing tools described below. Signing the Customer Contract, accepting the Terms or DPA, or acknowledging the Privacy Policy does not give cookie consent.

After a video loads, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to the cookies and data it uses.

**Links to other services**

Our WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and X buttons are plain links. They load nothing from those services on our website and set no cookies. Those services apply their own privacy and cookie policies once you open them.

Logging out immediately invalidates that session and its issued tokens; changing or resetting a password invalidates all sessions and their issued tokens. These are authentication rules, not confirmation of the lifetime or name of a browser cookie. The Hub uses no analytics, advertising or tracking technologies, so it shows no cookie banner. Our fonts are served from our own server, so your browser does not connect to Google Fonts.

# 6. Deleting and blocking cookies

You can delete cookies and local storage or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking necessary authentication cookies may prevent Hub login or normal operation. Clearing local storage may remove an unsaved draft. Deleting browser cookies does not itself delete personal data already held by Growth Rocket or a provider, and it does not necessarily communicate withdrawal to us. Use Cookie settings to withdraw optional consent; the Privacy Policy explains how to request deletion of personal data.
