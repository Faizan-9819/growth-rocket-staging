# 7. Your rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- access your data (Art. 15 GDPR)

- have it corrected (Art. 16)

- have it erased (Art. 17)

- restrict how it's used (Art. 18)

- receive it in a portable format (Art. 20)

- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that happened before (Art. 7(3)). For cookies, use "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page of our website.

**Your right to object (Art. 21 GDPR). Where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)), you can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then stop, unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or we need the data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. You can always object to direct marketing, and we will then stop using your data for it. To object, emailsupport@getgrowthrocket.com.**

To exercise your rights, email support@getgrowthrocket.com. We respond without undue delay and within one month. Where necessary because of complexity or the number of requests, we may extend this by up to two further months; we tell you within the first month and explain why. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity if we have reasonable doubts. For Customer Data processed on behalf of a business using Growth Rocket, contact that business; we assist it under the DPA.

You can complain to a data protection authority, especially in the EU country where you live or work, or where you believe a breach occurred. In the Netherlands, this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens: https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

# 8. Cookies

Our website uses analytics and advertising-measurement technologies only with the required consent. You can change or withdraw that consent through Cookie settings. The Hub's authentication and functional storage are separate from website analytics. The customer review form records its first opening without an email pixel, cookies or other device storage for that record, and without recording IP or device details in that record; separate server request logs may contain IP addresses. Our customers' notices explain their review processing. See our Cookie Policy at https://legal.getgrowthrocket.com/cookie-policy. Our cookie banner lets you accept all, reject all or choose per category, and rejecting is as easy as accepting. Until you choose, and if you reject, no analytics or advertising tools load. The Hub uses only strictly necessary cookies, plus a cookie that remembers your sidebar setting and local storage that keeps unsaved blog drafts. It uses no analytics or advertising technologies and shows no cookie banner.

# 9. Security

We protect personal data with appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

- EU-based primary hosting and file storage, with global processing and remote access as explained in section 5

- encrypted connections

- hashed passwords

- Account-based separation in a shared database, with checks that restrict customer requests to the relevant account's records

- Access limited to authorised people according to their role, with confidentiality commitments, access reviews and protected backups. Provider accounts use two-factor authentication; the Hub and admin dashboard do not currently offer two-factor sign-in.

Our DPA Annex II describes the service's security measures, including encryption in transit and the systems with encryption at rest. It does not claim that every log, queue or cache has additional encryption at rest. See Annex II of our DPA at https://legal.getgrowthrocket.com/dpa.
