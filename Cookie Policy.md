# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains which cookies and similar technologies Parashift Technologies B.V. ("Growth Rocket", "we") uses on its website `getgrowthrocket.com` and in the Growth Rocket Hub (`hub.getgrowthrocket.com`), and how you control them.

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage and tracking scripts, work in comparable ways. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

## 2. Your choice

- When you first visit `getgrowthrocket.com`, a banner asks whether you accept **analytics** and **marketing** cookies. You can accept all, reject all, or choose per category. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.
- **Until you choose, and if you reject, we only use strictly necessary cookies.** No analytics or advertising tools load.
- You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with **"Cookie settings"** at the bottom of every page. We ask again after 6 months.
- We don't need your consent for strictly necessary cookies (Art. 11.7a(3) Dutch Telecommunicatiewet, and the equivalent rules in other countries, such as § 25(2) German TDDDG).

## 3. Cookies on `getgrowthrocket.com`

**Strictly necessary** (always on)

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Expires |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consent cookie | Growth Rocket | Remembers your cookie choice | 6 months |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Growth Rocket | Remembers the language of the site | 1 year |

**Analytics** (only if you accept)

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Expires |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd.) | Distinguishes visitors, so we can count visits and see which pages are used | 2 years |
| `_ga_12W07KV2LM` | Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd.) | Keeps track of the current visit | 2 years |
| `_clck` | Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd.) | Recognises a returning visitor in Clarity | 1 year |
| `_clsk` | Microsoft Clarity | Links the pages of one visit into one recording | 1 day |
| `CLID`, `MUID` (set on Microsoft's domains) | Microsoft Clarity | Recognise a visitor across websites that use Clarity | about 1 year |

Microsoft Clarity records how visitors click, scroll and move around our pages, so we can see where the site is confusing. It hides everything typed into forms. Clarity recordings are kept for 30 days. Google Analytics data is kept for 2 months.

**Marketing** (only if you accept)

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Expires |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_gcl_au` | Google Ads (Google Ireland Ltd.) | Measures whether our ads lead to enquiries | 90 days |
| `_gcl_aw` | Google Ads (Google Ireland Ltd.) | Stores the ad-click ID when you arrive from one of our ads | 90 days |

With your consent, we also tell Google Ads when you submit our contact form or tap our phone number, so we can see which ads work. We don't send the content of the form.

**External media**

Our feature videos are hosted on YouTube. They load only when you click play, from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (`youtube-nocookie.com`). Once a video plays, YouTube (Google Ireland Ltd.) stores cookies and similar data under its own privacy policy.

**Links to other services**

Our WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and X buttons are plain links. They set no cookies on our site. Those services apply their own policies once you open them.

## 4. Cookies in the Growth Rocket Hub

The Hub uses only cookies it needs to work, plus two that remember choices you make while using it. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies in the Hub, so it shows no cookie banner. Our fonts are served from our own server, so your browser doesn't connect to Google Fonts.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Expires |
|---|---|---|---|
| `__Secure-next-auth.session-token` | Cookie, strictly necessary | Keeps you logged in | 7 days |
| `__Host-next-auth.csrf-token` | Cookie, strictly necessary | Protects the login form against cross-site request forgery | End of browser session |
| `__Secure-next-auth.callback-url` | Cookie, strictly necessary | Returns you to the right page after logging in | End of browser session |
| `sidebar_state` | Cookie, set only when you open or close the sidebar | Remembers the sidebar setting you chose | 7 days |
| `blog_draft_<id>` | Local storage on your device, set only while you write a blog post | Keeps an unsaved blog draft if the page closes | Until you save or discard the draft |

The Growth Rocket API (`api.getgrowthrocket.com`) sets no cookies.

## 5. Websites of businesses that use Growth Rocket

Growth Rocket's website templates set no cookies for visitors and load no third-party content before you consent. Each business is responsible for anything it adds to its own website, and explains it in its own privacy or cookie notice.

## 6. Deleting and blocking cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block the Hub's cookies, you won't be able to log in.
